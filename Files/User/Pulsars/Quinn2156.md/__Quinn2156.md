# Nature - Experiments in Pulsar Based Navigation 
===
**J. Quinn - Published 2156 Aug 6**

*Abstract*
Calibration of Universal Positioning System beacons has relied on consensus methods,
however, these methods are prone to errors and drift, resulting in numerous ships
going off course. We propose a method of calibration using observed pulsar positions
computed via their spindown timings.

*Methodology*
The slowdown of pulsar spin is incredibly precise and consistent. Given just the
spin derivative, and spin at a known date, along with orbital parameters, the
exact position of a pulsar can be determined. Since measured spin and derivative
are essentially unique to each pulsar, simple identification methods can be used.
